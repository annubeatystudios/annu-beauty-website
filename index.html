Meet the Founder

Ashayla Anu — Founder & CEO

Ashayla rented a suite in Atlanta as a loctician and rock stylist for years before she ever thought about owning the building. She knows exactly what suite rental is missing — the fees that aren't in the contract, the landlord who never asks how business is going, the isolation of building alone — because she lived it first.

So she built the thing she wished existed: a luxury studio space with real support behind it. Our mission is simple — give beauty and wellness professionals an affordable, luxury environment where they can launch, run, and grow thriving businesses, without going it alone.
